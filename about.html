About Bangali Gamer

Operating a mod developer team creating custom packages for football simulation games since December 2024.

Who We Are

Bangali Gamer is a Bangladesh-based FIFA & EA SPORTS FC mod development team with one clear mission — to bring Bangladesh to the world of football gaming. 🇧🇩

From FIFA 22 to EA SPORTS FC 26 and beyond, we have been working since December 2024 to bring authentic Bangladeshi football culture, identity, and passion into the game.

Our goal is not limited to a single game or a single version. We aim to continuously develop and expand the Bangladeshi football experience across future generations of FIFA & EA SPORTS FC.

From Bangladesh National Team and local leagues to players, kits, stadiums, chants, commentary, presentation, and unique football culture — we strive to make Bangladesh feel truly at home in the game.

We are not just creating mods.
We are building Bangladesh's presence in football gaming — today, tomorrow, and for the future. 🇧🇩

FIFA 22 → FC 26 → Beyond.
Built in Bangladesh. Driven by Football. Made for the Future.
